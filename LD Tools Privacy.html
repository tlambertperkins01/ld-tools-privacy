LD Tools — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

LD Tools is a macOS application for lighting designers that parses production documents (schedules, run-of-show, technical notes) into organized, editable views. This policy explains how it handles your information. In short: your documents stay on your Mac, and the app does not collect, track, or sell personal data.

Information we collect

None. LD Tools does not collect personal information, does not include analytics or tracking, and does not send your documents or their contents to us or to any third party for our benefit.

On-device processing

All document import, parsing, text extraction, and AI-assisted structuring happen locally on your Mac. Production documents you import are read and processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers — we do not operate any servers that receive your content.

Network activity

The only network request the app makes on its own is a one-time download of the on-device AI model from a public model host (Hugging Face) the first time the enhanced document features are used. This download transfers the model to your Mac; it does not transmit any of your document content. After the model is downloaded, the enhanced features run fully offline.

Optional Airtable integration

If you choose to connect the app to Airtable, the data you sync and the Airtable API token you provide are sent directly from your Mac to Airtable’s service over an encrypted connection, at your request, to perform the sync you initiate. Your API token is stored securely in the macOS Keychain on your device. This feature is entirely optional; if you do not connect Airtable, no such data is sent. Data handled by Airtable is subject to Airtable’s own privacy policy.

Data storage

Your shows, imported documents, and settings are stored locally on your Mac. You can delete this data at any time from within the app or by removing the application.

Children’s privacy

LD Tools is a professional tool and is not directed to children.
