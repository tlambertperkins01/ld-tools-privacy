# Sale timing: announced and historical deals

## US retail calendar (approximate)

| Window | Event | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Late Jan to Feb | Post-holiday clearance, Presidents' Day | Appliances, mattresses, cookware |
| May | Memorial Day | Appliances, mattresses, outdoor |
| Jul | Amazon Prime Day + rival events (Target Circle Week, Walmart Deals) | Electronics, kitchen, Amazon-brand items |
| Early Sep | Labor Day | Appliances, mattresses |
| Early Oct | Prime Big Deal Days | Electronics, kitchen |
| Late Nov | Black Friday → Cyber Monday (early deals from early Nov) | Nearly everything; strongest for cookware, TVs, small appliances |
| Mid Dec | Last-minute and Green Monday | Leftover doorbusters |

Brand-specific events matter too: friends-and-family sales (Sur La Table,
Macy's, Williams Sonoma), factory-seconds events (All-Clad VIP sale), and brand
anniversary sales.

## Announced deals (this year)

- Retailer ad scans and early-deal pages (Costco, Target, Walmart, Macy's and Kohl's
  ad leaks: bfads.net, blackfriday.com, deal-news sites).
- Brand sale pages with dates (e.g. `/blackfriday`, `/sale`).
- Dated deal articles from credible outlets (Wirecutter Deals, CNN Underscored
  Deals, NBC Select, The Kitchn, USA Today Reviewed, Slickdeals front page).
- Label a deal "confirmed" only if you saw it on the retailer's own page or ad, or in a dated
  article from one of those outlets.

## Historical lows

- Price trackers: camelcamelcamel / Keepa (Amazon), and the brand's own
  "lowest price in 30 days" notes where shown.
- Slickdeals threads, searched by exact model number, with dates.
- Prior-year deal roundups from the outlets above.

## Traps

- **SKU drift**: a past "$249 10-piece" may have been a different line (a nonstick
  version, an "Everyday" line, a store exclusive) or a price error that got
  cancelled. Match model numbers before citing a historical low.
- **Inflated "was" prices** during events. Compare against the tracker's history.
- **Benchmark moves too**: check whether the item you're trying to beat also goes on sale.
- **Price-adjustment policies**: many retailers refund the difference within
  14–30 days, but many suspend this during holiday events. Check before
  telling the user to buy now and claim the difference later.

## Verdict format

`Candidate · likely below target this event? high/med/low · where · evidence`

Then give a buy-now vs wait call, with the date the deal is expected to start.
