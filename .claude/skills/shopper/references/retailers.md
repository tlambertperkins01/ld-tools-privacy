# Retailers to sweep (US default; edit freely)

Cover **both** big and small. Small/specialty retailers often have codes the
big ones don't, or run the brand's own sale early. Pin favorites via `SHOP_STORES`.

## Big-box / mass

Amazon · Walmart · Target · Costco · Sam's Club · BJ's · Home Depot · Lowe's ·
Best Buy · Kohl's · Macy's · JCPenney · Wayfair · Overstock / Bed Bath & Beyond
(online) · QVC / HSN (bundles, Easy Pay) · Kroger / Meijer (seasonal)

## Department / premium

Nordstrom · Bloomingdale's · Neiman Marcus · Saks · Dillard's · Belk

## Kitchen & home specialty (big)

Williams Sonoma · Sur La Table · Crate & Barrel · Food52 · Zola (registry
pricing) · Bloomingdale's Home · Pottery Barn (some cookware)

## Kitchen & home specialty (small / independent)

Cutlery and More · Everything Kitchens · Kitchen Kapers · Abt Electronics ·
Chef's Arsenal · Cook's Warehouse · Over the Moon · Scheels · Verdi ·
Kitchen Collection-style outlets · local restaurant-supply sites
(WebstaurantStore, KaTom; commercial lines only)

## Electronics specialty

B&H Photo · Adorama · Crutchfield · Micro Center · Newegg (check seller) ·
Abt

## Brand direct, outlets, and seconds

- Brand site (often first with sales; email signup codes 10–20%)
- Factory seconds / outlet / "scratch & dent" / refurbished programs. Examples:
  All-Clad VIP sale (homeandcooksales.com), Zwilling/Henckels outlet,
  Le Creuset outlet, Apple/Dyson/Breville refurbished. Note: often **final
  sale**, cosmetic flaws, sometimes shorter warranty. State these terms.
- Open-box: Best Buy, Amazon Resale (Warehouse).

## Marketplaces (judge the seller, not the storefront)

Amazon third-party · Walmart Marketplace · eBay (new/sealed, top-rated seller
only) · Target Plus

## Notes on blocking (observed)

Amazon, Walmart, Target, Macy's, Williams Sonoma, Kohl's, Lowe's, Wirecutter,
Good Housekeeping and Serious Eats commonly block plain fetchers (403/451/503
or "unauthorized AI agent"). Use a real browser if available; otherwise cite
search snippets labelled `snippet`. Don't hammer a site that has refused you.
